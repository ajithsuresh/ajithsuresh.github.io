About

I lead MPC research at the Cryptography Research Center of the Technology Innovation Institute (TII), Abu Dhabi, designing and building applied secure multiparty computation protocols for privacy-preserving machine learning and federated learning.

Before TII I spent 1.5 years as a post-doctoral researcher in the ENCRYPTO group at TU Darmstadt with Prof. Thomas Schneider. I earned my M.Tech (Research) and Ph.D. at the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, with Prof. Arpita Patra, and my B.Tech at the College of Engineering, Trivandrum.
